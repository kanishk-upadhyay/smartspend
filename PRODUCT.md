Product: SmartSpend

Summary:
SmartSpend helps individuals and small teams automatically ingest receipts and transactions, extract structured expense data, categorize spending, and visualize financial trends. It prioritizes fast receipt scanning, lightweight analytics, and easy manual correction.

Users:
- Busy professionals tracking receipts for taxes and expense reports
- Freelancers and small teams managing reimbursable expenses

Goals:
- Accurate OCR ingestion and minimal friction to add expenses
- Clear, actionable visuals for spending trends and allocation
- Offline-first, privacy-respecting storage with optional cloud sync

Tone & constraints:
- Product (not marketing) register: pragmatic, trustworthy, concise
- Dark theme preferred for prolonged use in low-light environments
- Accessible (WCAG AA), keyboard-friendly, readable typography

Anti-references:
- Avoid SaaS marketing fluff, gradient-heavy UI, and modal-first flows.

Reference:
- Main UI: frontend/src/App.tsx
